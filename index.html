What we corrected from the source courses

Custom GPTs are on the way out

One course builds its sales tools as custom GPTs. OpenAI now plans to retire them on 11 December 2026, so this guide uses projects, skills and plugins instead.

AI-voice outbound calls need consent

The speed-to-lead and reactivation upsells call people with an AI voice. In the US that requires the person's prior express consent. The course doesn't say so; this guide does.

Sales pitches and income claims left out

Both sources include paid programs, giveaways and personal earnings figures. None of that is here, and industry statistics are flagged to verify before you quote them.
